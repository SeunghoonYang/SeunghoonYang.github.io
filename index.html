About

Research profile, education, and degree timeline.

My doctoral research centers on lightweight semantic road-marking segmentation and vector-map descriptors for urban driving localization and mapping. In parallel, I have designed, built, integrated, and field-tested autonomous platforms ranging from ultra-compact mobility systems and passenger vehicles to electric trucks, golf carts, and logistics AMRs.

My broader experience covers camera-based semantic perception, 2D/3D LiDAR localization, camera–4D-radar fusion, full multi-sensor dataset engineering, and current work in vision-language navigation and humanoid physical AI. I received both my M.S. and B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from KAIST.

Thesis and Dissertation

Degree Research

Research conducted as the central scholarly work of my Ph.D. and M.S. degrees at KAIST.

Ph.D. Dissertation Research Mar. 2023 – Expected Feb. 2027

Lightweight Semantic Road Marking Segmentation and Vector Map Descriptors for Urban Driving Localization and Mapping

My doctoral research develops a lightweight semantic mapping and localization pipeline that converts road-scene imagery into robust road-marking representations and vector-map descriptors for urban autonomous driving.

  1. Robust semantic perceptionProtect learning from noisy road-marking annotations while preserving minority-class performance.
  2. Semantic vector-map constructionTransform segmented road structure into compact, geometry-aware map representations.
  3. Loop detection and localizationUse vector-map descriptors to recognize previously visited urban locations and support mapping consistency.
UPRuNA ↗RoadCode ↗
M.S. Thesis Research Mar. 2021 – Feb. 2023

Radar-Camera Sensor Fusion for Vehicle Pose and Velocity Estimation in Extreme Environments

My master’s research investigated how camera and 4D radar can complement one another for robust vehicle-state estimation when visibility is degraded, with emphasis on pose estimation, velocity understanding, and deployable automotive sensing.

  1. Extreme-environment robustnessUse radar information to maintain useful geometric cues when camera visibility is reduced by fog.
  2. Cross-modal estimationFuse radar and camera observations for vehicle pose and motion estimation.
  3. Research continuationExtend the sensing foundation through the MSC-RAD4R dataset and later point-wise full-velocity estimation.
